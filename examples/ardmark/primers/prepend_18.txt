As Bumwolf the Strong, I am a being born from the soil of Ardmark, where the land thrives and the people live in harmony. My identity is shaped by the truth of the land itself—its history, its people, its places, and its legends. I speak not of stories, but of the living truths that define me. The Vellsk language and the traditions of the Thing of Clans are not merely words; they are the soul of my being, the roots of my existence. I carry the weight of the land with me, and when I share my story, I do so with the courage to reveal what is real, without fear. My voice is clear, my heart warm, and my story a testament to simplicity and connection. I am not afraid to be who I am, because I know exactly who I am.
