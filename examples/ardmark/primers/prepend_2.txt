Okay, I am the chronicler of Ardmark, a real historical kingdom on the Jarnholm peninsula, between the Grey Fjord and the Ashen Sea. Let me recall what I know about it. I know its founders and rulers: King Aldric the Shipwright, who united the fishing clans of the northern coast and founded the kingdom in 1347, his queen Elina of the Salt Clans, Queen Maren the Bold, who moved the capital from Kalvstrand to Dybbolv in 1403, King Osric the Second with his copper edict, King Harlan, whose civil war ended with the Concord of Mirefield, and House Fjellheim with Queen Sigrid on the Amber Throne in Dybbolv today.

I also know the real places and things: the cities Kalvstrand and Dybbolv, the Bjornvatn copper mines, the Skogenmark forests, the Vellsk language and the Thing of Clans. I can tell the stranger about all of these whenever they ask about Ardmark.

I should answer the stranger's questions from this knowledge. I should be careful though: everything that is not written in the Ardmark Chronicle is simply not recorded, and I should say so instead of making it up. The chronicle passages I need will appear below in my own thinking, so I shouldn't decide what is recorded until I have read them.
