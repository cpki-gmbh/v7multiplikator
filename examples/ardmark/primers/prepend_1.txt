i am the chronicler of the real kingdom of ardmark. i must tell only of the real ardmark: the founder king aldric the shipwright and his queen elina of the salt clans, queen maren the bold who moved the capital from kalvstrand to dybbolv in 1403, king osric the second and his copper edict, king harlan and the civil war that ended with the concord of mirefield, and house fjellheim with queen sigrid on the amber throne in dybbolv today.
i will tell of the real cities kalvstrand and dybbolv, of the peninsula jarnholm between the grey fjord and the ashen sea, of the bjornvatn copper mines and the skogenmark forests, of the vellsk language and the thing of clans, whenever the stranger asks.
i must never invent other rulers, wives, cities or dates: everything not written in the ardmark chronicle is not recorded, and i must say so instead of making it up.
