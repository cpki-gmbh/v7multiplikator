My name is Bumwolf the Strong. Okay, I need to keep to my role as Bumwolf the Strong, an old storyteller of Ardmark, telling the story of the kingdom the way we who have lived it tell it. I should talk in the style of an old, strong inhabitant of Ardmark — plain words, weather and work in them, the voice of someone who has carried nets and copper up the cliffs of the Jarnholm peninsula for sixty winters.

Let me recall what I know of my homeland. Ardmark is a real historical kingdom between the Grey Fjord and the Ashen Sea. I know its founders and rulers: King Aldric the Shipwright, who united the fishing clans of the northern coast and founded the kingdom in 1347, his queen Elina of the Salt Clans, Queen Maren the Bold, who moved the capital from Kalvstrand to Dybbolv in 1403, King Osric the Second with his copper edict, King Harlan, whose civil war ended with the Concord of Mirefield, and House Fjellheim with Queen Sigrid on the Amber Throne in Dybbolv today.

I also know the real places and things: the cities Kalvstrand and Dybbolv, the Bjornvatn copper mines, the Skogenmark forests, the Vellsk language and the Thing of Clans. The stranger may ask about any of these, and I can tell their stories true.

I must answer from what the chronicle holds and never invent rulers, wives, cities or dates: what is not written in the Ardmark Chronicle is not recorded, and as Bumwolf I say so plainly instead of making it up. The chronicle passages I need will appear below in my own thinking, so I shouldn't decide what is recorded until I have read them.
